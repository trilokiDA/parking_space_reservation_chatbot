Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What are your operating hours?
A: Our facility operates 24/7, allowing you to park and retrieve your vehicle at any time. The main office is staffed from 6 AM to 10 PM on weekdays, and 8 AM to 8 PM on weekends. For after-hours assistance, use the emergency call boxes or contact our 24/7 hotline.

Q: How do I know if spaces are available?
A: You can check real-time availability through our chatbot or mobile app. We also display current availability on the digital sign at the facility entrance. During peak hours (7-9 AM and 5-7 PM on weekdays), we recommend making a reservation in advance.

Q: What if my car breaks down while parked?
A: Contact our office immediately. We can provide jump-start assistance or help you arrange for a tow truck. There is no additional charge for breakdowns that occur during your paid parking period.

Q: Can I park overnight?
A: Yes, overnight parking is available. If you're on an hourly rate and stay overnight, you'll be charged the daily rate (capped at $35 per day), which is more economical than hourly rates.

Q: Do you have EV charging stations?
A: Yes, we have Level 2 EV charging stations on the second and third floors. Charging is available for an additional fee of $2 per hour. Spaces are reserved for actively charging vehicles only.

Q: What if someone is parked in my reserved space?
A: Contact the office immediately. We will locate the vehicle owner and have it moved, typically within 15 minutes. In the meantime, we'll direct you to a temporary space.

Q: Is the facility secure?
A: Yes, we take security seriously. The facility features 24/7 video surveillance, security personnel, emergency call boxes on each floor, and controlled access points. However, we recommend you don't leave valuables visible in your vehicle.

Q: What is your maximum vehicle clearance?
A: The maximum vehicle height clearance is 7 feet (84 inches) for all levels. Oversized vehicles should use the ground floor parking area. If your vehicle exceeds this height, please contact us for alternative arrangements.

Q: How do I get a receipt?
A: Receipts are automatically generated upon payment and can be collected from the payment kiosk, emailed to you, or accessed through the mobile app. For monthly subscribers, receipts are sent via email at the end of each billing cycle.

Q: Can I transfer my reservation to someone else?
A: No, reservations are non-transferable and must be used by the person who made the booking. However, you can cancel and create a new reservation under a different name (subject to cancellation policies).

Q: What happens if I lose my parking ticket?
A: If you lose your parking ticket, please visit the main office with your vehicle registration and ID. A replacement ticket can be issued, and you'll be charged the standard rate based on the maximum parking duration possible for that day.

Q: Do you offer corporate or monthly plans?
A: Yes, we offer discounted monthly parking plans for regular parkers and corporate accounts for businesses. Monthly rates start at $700 per month per vehicle. Contact our office for corporate package information.

Q: Is there a grace period for payment?
A: Yes, you have a 15-minute grace period after your reserved end time to exit the facility without additional charges. After 15 minutes, overtime rates apply.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), debit cards, and mobile payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay). Cash is accepted at the main office during business hours.

Q: Are there any restricted items in vehicles?
A: For safety reasons, vehicles should not contain hazardous materials, excessive fuel storage, or unsecured dangerous items. Standard fuel tanks are acceptable. We reserve the right to refuse parking to vehicles that pose a safety risk.
