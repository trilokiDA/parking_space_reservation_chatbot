Parking Reservation Process

How to Make a Reservation:

Step 1: Provide Personal Information
To make a reservation, you'll need to provide:
- First name
- Last name
- Vehicle registration/license plate number

Step 2: Select Parking Period
Choose your desired parking period by specifying:
- Start date and time
- End date and time

Reservations can be made for:
- Hourly parking (minimum 1 hour)
- Daily parking (full day rate)
- Weekly parking (7-day period)
- Monthly parking (30-day period)

Step 3: Confirmation Process
After submitting your reservation:
1. You will receive a reservation ID
2. Your reservation will be marked as "pending"
3. A parking administrator will review and confirm your reservation
4. You will be notified once confirmed (typically within 1-2 hours)
5. You will receive your assigned parking space number

Step 4: Arrival
When you arrive:
- Enter through the main entrance
- Proceed to your assigned parking space
- Display your reservation confirmation if requested
- Park only in your designated space

Reservation Policies:

Cancellation Policy:
- Reservations can be cancelled up to 24 hours before start time for a full refund
- Cancellations within 24 hours: 50% refund
- No-shows: no refund

Modification Policy:
- Reservations can be modified up to 12 hours before start time
- Changes to vehicle registration number must be made before arrival
- Date/time changes subject to availability

Late Arrival:
- Your space is held for 2 hours past the start time
- After 2 hours, the reservation may be cancelled without refund
- Please notify us if you'll be delayed

Early Departure:
- No refunds for early departure
- Please notify the office when leaving early

Extension:
- Parking extensions subject to availability
- Contact the office or use the mobile app to request an extension
- Extension rates apply based on original booking rate

Important Notes:
- One reservation per vehicle at a time
- The vehicle registration number must match the parked vehicle
- Reservations are non-transferable
- Smoking is not permitted in the facility
- Maximum vehicle height: 7 feet
- Commercial vehicles require special arrangements
