General Parking Information

Our parking facility is a modern, secure parking structure located in the heart of the downtown business district. We offer convenient parking solutions for both short-term and long-term needs.

Location:
The parking facility is located at 123 Main Street, Downtown District. The entrance is accessible from Main Street with an additional exit on Oak Avenue. The facility is within walking distance of major office buildings, shopping centers, and public transportation hubs.

Facility Features:
- Multi-level covered parking structure
- 24/7 security surveillance with CCTV cameras
- Well-lit parking areas for enhanced safety
- Electric vehicle (EV) charging stations available
- Handicap-accessible parking spaces on each level
- Elevator and stair access to all floors
- Automated payment kiosks

Parking Space Types:
- Standard parking spaces (9 feet wide)
- Compact car spaces (8 feet wide)
- Oversized vehicle spaces (available on ground floor)
- Reserved monthly parking spaces
- EV charging spots (Level 2 chargers)
- Handicap-accessible spaces near elevators

Security and Safety:
Our facility employs state-of-the-art security measures including 24/7 video surveillance, security personnel on-site, emergency call boxes on each level, and well-maintained fire safety systems. The entire facility is well-lit and regularly patrolled.

Payment Methods:
We accept various payment methods including credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), debit cards, mobile payment apps (Apple Pay, Google Pay), and monthly subscription plans.

Contact Information:
For assistance or inquiries, please contact:
- Phone: Main office (available 24/7)
- Email: support@parking-facility.com
- Emergency: Use call boxes located on each floor
